My way into AI came through engineering: a B.Sc. in Industrial Engineering from TU Berlin, and a bachelor's thesis on predictive maintenance for autonomous delivery robots.

### 🔭 What I work on

- **[Skill++](https://github.com/skill-plus-plus-org/skill-plus-plus)**, co-creator. It watches Claude Code sessions on your own machine and turns the procedures you repeat into reviewed agent skills. Local models, no tokens, measured on 30 public recordings.
- **Agentic onboarding assistant** (internal, Concept Reply). Google ADK and CopilotKit over AG-UI, live company knowledge through MCP, and an evaluation harness that combines contract tests with live behavioral scoring.
- **Three-agent proof of concept** for an industrial technology client: device-data standardization (ECLASS, AAS submodels), portfolio matching for sales quotes, and device-configuration tracking.
- **Teaching:** a seven-session course for developers on agentic AI with Google ADK, MCP servers and Ollama.
